BILLING AND PAYMENTS

Invoice Enquiries
If you have a question about a charge on your account, please log in and navigate to
Billing > Invoice History. Each invoice lists itemised charges. If something looks
incorrect, click "Dispute Charge" to raise a ticket.

Refund Policy
Refunds for returned items are processed within 5–7 business days after the warehouse
receives and inspects the product. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.
Gift card payments are refunded as store credit.

Subscription Billing
Our Premium Support subscription ($9.99/month) renews automatically on the same date
each month. You can cancel at any time from Account > Subscriptions without penalty.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

Late Payment Fees
If your payment method fails, we retry after 3 days. A $5 late-payment fee applies
after 10 days of non-payment. Accounts more than 30 days overdue are suspended.

Tax Exemptions
Non-profit organisations and government entities may apply for tax exemption.
Email tax-exempt@example.com with your exemption certificate.
